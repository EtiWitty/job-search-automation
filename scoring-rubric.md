---

## How to Use This Rubric

1. **Check deal-breakers first** — if any are triggered, stop and reject the role
2. **Score each category** on a 1–10 scale using the criteria below
3. **Apply category weights** and calculate the weighted total
4. **Interpret the final score** using the thresholds at the bottom

---

## ⛔ Deal-Breaker Flags (Auto-Reject)

If ANY of the following are true, the role is disqualified regardless of score:

| # | Deal-Breaker | Check |
|---|---|---|
| DB-1 | No equity in compensation package | ☐ |
| DB-2 | No AI/LLM involvement in core work | ☐ |
| DB-3 | Required to be onsite daily or weekly | ☐ |
| DB-4 | Legacy tech stack (COBOL, mainframe, .NET Framework) | ☐ |
| DB-5 | No remote culture / no async infrastructure | ☐ |
| DB-6 | Defense or government contracting | ☐ |

> **If any box is checked → Score = 0 / REJECT. Do not proceed.**

## Scoring Categories

### 1. AI/LLM Technical Match (Weight: 25%)

Does the role involve meaningful, production-level AI/LLM work?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Core focus is LLM systems; prompt engineering, LLM evaluation, agent frameworks in production at scale |
| 7–8 | Significant AI/LLM work; building APIs on top of LLMs, integrating AI into product features |
| 5–6 | Some AI involvement; AI is part of roadmap but not yet central to the role |
| 3–4 | Minimal AI; occasional ML feature work, mostly traditional backend |
| 1–2 | No meaningful AI/LLM component; AI mentioned as buzzword only |

---

### 2. Compensation (Weight: 20%)

Does total comp (base + bonus + equity value) align with target of $180K–$220K?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Total comp clearly $220K+ with strong equity; top-of-market for stage |
| 7–8 | Total comp $180K–$220K; equity present and meaningful |
| 5–6 | Total comp $150K–$180K; equity present but limited; or $180K+ but equity is minimal |
| 3–4 | Total comp $120K–$150K; equity small or vague |
| 1–2 | Below $120K total comp; or no equity at all (also triggers DB-1) |

---

### 3. Remote Work (Weight: 15%)

Does the role support fully remote work?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Fully remote, async-first culture; distributed team; no required travel |
| 7–8 | Remote-first with optional in-person gatherings (1–2x/year offsite) |
| 5–6 | Hybrid with flexible days (e.g., 1 day/month or occasional visits) |
| 3–4 | Hybrid required 1–2 days/week; some flexibility |
| 1–2 | Mostly onsite with occasional remote days; or unclear |

> Note: Fully required onsite triggers DB-3 and is an auto-reject.

---

### 4. Company Stage & Equity Upside (Weight: 15%)

Does the company stage and equity structure offer meaningful upside?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Series B–C with strong growth trajectory; equity likely to be worth $500K+ at exit; recent fundraise |
| 7–8 | Series A–D with solid growth signals; equity meaningful; clear path to liquidity |
| 5–6 | Mid-size company (500–5K employees); equity present but exit is less clear |
| 3–4 | Public company with RSUs; predictable but limited upside |
| 1–2 | Pre-seed/bootstrapped (high risk, unclear equity), or large public co with negligible equity |

---

### 5. Industry Alignment (Weight: 10%)

Does the company operate in EdTech, HR Tech, or Fintech?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Core EdTech, HR Tech, or Career Tech product (e.g., learning platforms, workforce tools, professional development) |
| 7–8 | Fintech or adjacent (payments, financial services, lending, insurance tech) |
| 5–6 | B2B SaaS or developer tools with AI-native product; adjacent to target industries |
| 3–4 | General tech company with no specific industry fit; product is unrelated to interests |
| 1–2 | Industry actively undesirable (e.g., pure enterprise legacy, defense, ad tech) |

---

### 6. Tech Stack Match (Weight: 10%)

Does the tech stack align with expertise in Java, Python, distributed systems, and modern infrastructure?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Java or Python backend; Kafka or real-time data systems; AWS/GCP; modern CI/CD; AI-native tooling |
| 7–8 | Strong backend stack in a known language; cloud-native; some distributed systems |
| 5–6 | Modern stack but different primary language (e.g., Go, Rust) — learnable but not native |
| 3–4 | Heavy on unfamiliar technologies; significant ramp-up required |
| 1–2 | Legacy or proprietary stack with no modern tooling |

---

### 7. Cultural Fit (Weight: 5%)

Does the company culture align with preferences for low bureaucracy, ownership, and async communication?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Engineer-driven culture; fast iteration; strong async/docs culture; explicit developer empowerment |
| 7–8 | Good engineering culture with some process; engineers own their roadmap; healthy on-call practices |
| 5–6 | Average tech company culture; some ownership but also bureaucracy |
| 3–4 | Heavy process; slow deploys; engineers as executors not decision-makers |
| 1–2 | Toxic indicators; high turnover in engineering; engineering as cost center |

---

## Score Calculation

```
Final Score =
  (AI/LLM Match × 0.25)
+ (Compensation × 0.20)
+ (Remote Work × 0.15)
+ (Company Stage/Equity × 0.15)
+ (Industry Alignment × 0.10)
+ (Tech Stack Match × 0.10)
+ (Cultural Fit × 0.05)
```

### Example

| Category | Score | Weight | Weighted |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI/LLM Technical Match | 8 | 25% | 2.00 |
| Compensation | 7 | 20% | 1.40 |
| Remote Work | 10 | 15% | 1.50 |
| Company Stage & Equity | 8 | 15% | 1.20 |
| Industry Alignment | 9 | 10% | 0.90 |
| Tech Stack Match | 8 | 10% | 0.80 |
| Cultural Fit | 7 | 5% | 0.35 |
| **Total** | | **100%** | **8.15** |

---

## Score Interpretation

| Final Score | Action |
|---|---|
| **8.5 – 10.0** | 🟢 **Top Priority** — Apply immediately; prioritize cover letter and outreach |
| **7.0 – 8.4** | 🟡 **Strong Match** — Apply; worth personalizing materials |
| **5.5 – 6.9** | 🟠 **Possible** — Apply only if pipeline is thin; low customization effort |
| **Below 5.5** | 🔴 **Skip** — Not worth applying |

> **Minimum threshold for shortlist: 7.0**
