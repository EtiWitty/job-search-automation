# Job Search Criteria — Aleksandar Cakic

_Last updated: 2026-05-26_

---

## Target Roles

**Primary titles:**
- Senior Software Engineer (Backend / AI)
- Senior Backend Engineer — AI/ML Platform
- Senior Engineer — LLM / Generative AI
- Senior Engineer — Developer Tools / AI Infrastructure

**Seniority level:** Senior Engineer (IC track). Not targeting management or staff/principal at this stage, but open to staff if the scope is right.

**Not targeting:** Engineering Manager, Frontend Engineer, Full-Stack generalist roles.

---

## Compensation Expectations

| Component | Target |
|---|---|
| Base salary | $160K – $200K |
| Total comp (base + bonus + equity) | $180K – $220K |
| Equity | Required — meaningful upside, not token grants |
| Bonus | Nice-to-have; equity matters more |

**Non-negotiable:** Equity must be part of the package. No equity = automatic disqualification.

---

## Location & Remote Preferences

- **Preference:** Fully remote
- **Acceptable:** Async-first or remote-friendly with optional in-person (< 1x/month)
- **Not acceptable:** Required onsite or hybrid with mandatory office days
- **Geography:** Open to US-based remote roles; not seeking to relocate

---

## Company Size & Stage

**Preferred (in order):**
1. Growth-stage startup (Series C+) — scaling teams, fast-moving, meaningful equity
2. Early-stage startup (Series A–B) — greenfield, high ownership, high upside
3. Mid-size tech company (500–5,000 employees) — established product, modern stack

**Not targeting:** Big tech / FAANG (e.g., Meta, Google, Amazon, Apple). Open to ex-FAANG spinouts or small product teams within larger orgs if the culture is startup-like.

---

## Industry Focus

**Preferred industries:**
- **EdTech / HR Tech / Career Tech** — Natural fit given LinkedIn background and career coaching practice. Deep domain knowledge in professional development, learning, and workforce tools.
- **Fintech** — Interest in financial services, payments, or banking tech. Experience with sensitive data handling (PII, credit data, compliance) is directly relevant.

**Open to:** AI infrastructure, developer tools, B2B SaaS with AI-native products.

**Avoid:** Pure enterprise software with no AI angle, legacy financial systems, defense/government contracting.

---

## Technical Must-Haves

The role must involve active use or development of:
- **LLM / Generative AI** systems (OpenAI, Anthropic, or internal models)
- Modern backend stack (Java, Python, or similar — not COBOL, .NET legacy, etc.)
- RESTful or GraphQL API design at scale
- Cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, or Azure)

**Strong preference for roles involving:**
- Prompt engineering or LLM evaluation frameworks
- High-throughput distributed systems (Kafka, real-time data pipelines)
- Production AI systems with real user scale (100K+ users)

---

## Nice-to-Have Skills / Role Features

- LangChain, LangGraph, or agent framework experience
- Startup experience or founding-team proximity
- Clear growth path to Staff Engineer
- Engineering culture that values developer experience and tooling
- Overlap with career coaching / professional development domain

---

## Deal-Breakers (Auto-Reject)

Any of the following disqualifies a role immediately:

- [ ] **No equity** — cash-only compensation packages
- [ ] **No AI/LLM involvement** — roles with no meaningful AI component
- [ ] **Fully on-site required** — any role requiring daily or weekly office presence
- [ ] **Legacy tech stack** — COBOL, mainframe, .NET Framework, or equivalent
- [ ] **No remote culture** — company has no infrastructure or culture for async/remote work
- [ ] **Defense / government contracting** — not aligned with background or interests

---

## Cultural Fit Factors

**Strong yes:**
- Fast feedback loops, low bureaucracy
- Engineers have real ownership over product decisions
- Culture of documentation and async communication
- Team values AI tooling and developer productivity (Cursor, Copilot, etc.)
- Psychological safety and direct feedback culture

**Yellow flags:**
- >6-month release cycles
- Heavy process overhead for simple deploys
- No clear engineering ladder or growth framework

**Red flags:**
- "We move fast and break things" without reliability culture
- No on-call rotation plan (or chaotic on-call)
- Engineering as a cost center, not a product driver
